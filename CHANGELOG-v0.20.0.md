# Grey Ghost v0.20.0

## Progression & economy

- Realm character creation, viewing, and renaming.
- Character training in **Health**, **Damage**, and **Resistance**.
- `/daily claim` awards **2–5 coins** every 24 hours.
- `/daily train` trains one stat every 24 hours.
- `/coin balance` and `/coin history`.
- Five mount tiers and five armour tiers priced at **1 / 15 / 50 / 100 / 150** coins.
- `/shop`, `/buy`, `/inventory`, `/stable`, `/armoury`, and `/loadout`.
- New persistent `data/economy.json` store.
- Server backups upgraded to v2 so economy data is included; v1 backups remain accepted.
- Help/about/version metadata updated to v0.20.0.

## Reserved for v0.21.0

Equipment upgrades, injuries, recovery, bandages, ransom/spoils, and equipment integration into jousting are intentionally not included yet.
