# Grey Ghost v0.26.0

## Stables & Hunts

- Added `/setup event-channel` for one fixed competition-entry channel used by published jousts, grand melees, horse races, and future seasonal festivals. Tourney Summons pings remain outside embeds.
- Expanded `/stable` with horse records, customization, daily Speed/Stamina/Agility training, Bond, race records, and hunting records.
- Added `/race` with Sprint, Distance, Cross-Country, and Grand Race formats. Top-three finishers receive the configured Champions role, use the Admirer prize framework, and gain Renown.
- Added `/hunt go` and `/hunt stats`. Hunts are available every 12 hours and can reward coins/Renown while advancing an equipped horse's field experience.
- Added dragon lairs through `/dragon lair`, with persistent name, location, and description stored in the Dragon Registry.
- Backup format v6 includes stable, race, horse, and hunt data while older backup formats remain supported.
