# Grey Ghost v0.29.7

## New allegiance panels

- Added **The Trial of Seven** as a configurable single-choice self-role panel.
  - Intended sides: **Aerion's Champions** and **Dunk's Champions**.
- Added **The Fossoway Apples** as a configurable single-choice self-role panel.
  - Intended sides: **The Red Apples of Cider Hall** and **The Green Apples of New Barrel**.
- The new panels work with the existing `/selfroles configure`, `/selfroles emoji`, `/selfroles publish`, `/selfroles view`, and `/selfroles clear` tools.
- Realm statistics now publish dedicated allegiance-count pages for these panels when configured.
- Realm profiles automatically show configured Trial of Seven and Fossoway allegiance roles.
