# Grey Ghost v0.23.0

## Expeditions

- Added multi-stage cooperative expeditions with Easy, Standard, and Perilous difficulty.
- Added `/expedition create`, `join`, `leave`, `choose`, `status`, and `continue`.
- Expedition outcomes use party size, character training, mounts, armour, supplies, player choices, and controlled randomness.
- Added coin rewards, the Expedition Veteran achievement, flawless-run cosmetics, and optional Expedition Pathfinder role handling.
- Successful discoveries can be published to the Chronicles.
- Expedition participation can advance active Service to the Realm House quests.
- Failed stages may inflict temporary bruised/wounded injuries only.
- Added expedition data to backup format v4 while preserving restore support for v1-v3 backups.
- Added cosmetic inventory display and expedition cosmetic persistence.
